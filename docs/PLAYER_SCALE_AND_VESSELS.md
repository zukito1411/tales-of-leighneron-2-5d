# Player scale, coastal vessels and the Magus tower

Building height is fitted once during scenery registration to the 76-unit standing hero. Doors have at least 30% vertical headroom; human and elven cottage doors are also taller and wider within their existing walls. Other single-building doors are widened. Building X/Z dimensions, static collision, map lots, entry IDs, doors, NPC paths and saved coordinates remain unchanged. This intentionally grows elevations instead of expanding lots into adjacent streets. The per-instance height transform pivots around the original foundation and is shared by solid/additive rendering and projected shadows. Attached lights and broad-phase bounds account for the new height. No per-house geometry copy or texture is created.

Normal docked, ambient and passage boats share a single 3D prefab through the existing furniture instance pipeline. It has a closed clinker hull, continuous gunwale, deck, benches, mast, rigging, rudder and barrels. The triangular cream sail uses explicit UVs into frame zero of the original prepared coastal-boat artwork. No additional runtime image or upload is needed. World route heading rotates the hull independently of camera yaw. Crew and passengers retain their original complete sprite poses at actual deck positions, with water-level height and bobbing shared by the hull and crew. Deck shadows use the fixed water height. The old flat rail overlay is hidden only in 3D mode. The model remains within the original 190-unit boat route clearance; route planning, travel timing and save state are unchanged.

The Tower of Might (the Grand Magus trial) follows tower-of-might-v2.png: round stone shaft, four floors, gold stone collars, pointed purple windows, rune hangings, buttresses and a crenellated crown with small capped turrets. Structural masonry uses a surveyed stone-only patch; windows and rune panels reuse individual original details. Its southern portal begins at ground level. The original exterior and collider stay fixed. TOWER_APPROACH places the interaction target 48 units south of the old TOWER_DOOR anchor, clearing the full feet rectangle; site entries, floor definitions, map markers and quest guidance share that standing point. InteriorSession's existing clear-point, clear-path, proximity, campaign, boss and weekly progression guards are retained.

Compilation does not establish device performance or full-world gameplay correctness. Browser review is staged separately from the release tree, using a fresh save and explicitly recorded campaign prerequisites to inspect the tower. No player save or production unlock rule is changed by that staging.
